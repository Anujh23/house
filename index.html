Open heart · ~465 sqft

One long, low, warm room

Foyer → dining → hall → living → the D, all one 11′ room. No full walls and no column: just a rounded stub wall, the kitchen's ledge with a fluted slider, and soft arches. From the front door you look ~19′ diagonally to the D, and through it to the lit niche wall ~25′ away.

North side

Sleeping-D + north court

A 17′ × 10′ D lying on its flat side: the curve rises almost from the floor, like a half-moon. Only 3 slim bronze bars; the middle pair lifts and slides onto the shaded north court; the two glass panes above the doors are top-hung vents. The D frames a planted court (~5′ deep) closed by a 7′ Oat-limewash wall with its own small arched niche, two uplights and an olive tree on the D's axis.

Cozy beige

Warm, not empty

Oat limewash, beige Kota floors, a honey-teak batten band over a curved cream sofa, Kota benches in the D, an otla bench on the porch, a window seat in your room, 2700K light, cane and plants.

Parents SW · you above

Stacked suites

Bedroom 1 (parents) in the SW with an elder-friendly wet room. Your master sits right on top: 10′ south glass with a window seat, a study at the north glass, your own bath, and a glass door to the open NW terrace.

Kitchen · SE

Cook faces east

15.5′ × 12.5′. Hob on the east counter, sink under the east window, fridge and pantry on the west. An arch plus a 3′6″ ledge with a fluted slider opens it to the dining, but the front door can't see the hob.

Terraces · ~870 sqft

Outdoor rooms

An open NW terrace off the study (planters, cane sofa, a charpai; no pergola), one flat ~20′ × 18′ north deck for charpai nights (no curbs or steps to trip on), a SE sunrise deck (add a shade sail later if you want one). The roof over the mandir is kept bare and fenced off by a low kerb.

Why it feels open

Open without a double-height void

  • One 465 sqft heart with no full-height walls and no column: one 24′ beam at the living–dining seam carries the roof.
  • Long sightlines: ~19′ from the front door to the D (~25′ to the north niche wall); 24′ from the foyer to the lobby arch.
  • Width instead of height: 24′ wide living, a 17′ D rising from the floor, lantern light over the dining.
  • Bedrooms of 175, 175 and 140 sqft (+38 sqft walk-through dressing), no corridors: one 7′ × 6′ lobby serves both ground bedrooms.
  • Built-ins instead of loose furniture: D benches, TV niche, bedside niches, otla, window seat.
  • Three-layer thresholds: living → D reveal → garden; foyer → porch → yard; master → study → open terrace.
  • Doors only on bedrooms, baths and the store. Everything else is an arch or open.

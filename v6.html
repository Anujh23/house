Vastu-first layout

Parents SW, you right above

Bedroom 1 (parents) sits in the SW with a walk-in dressing room and an attached bathroom. Your master is stacked exactly on top, the same as Bhai's house: same walls, dressing and bath above dressing and bath, so the plumbing runs straight down.

NE · living + mandir

Double-height living + Portal D

The living is in the light NE corner, 22′ high. Its glass is a Portal D: half-round top, only 3 slim bars, set 2′ back inside a deep wood frame. The bottom slides open onto a shaded north patio and the top vents tilt open, so the room pulls in cool north air and lets hot air out at the top. The mandir sits in the NE corner with nothing above it.

Your room

Big south glass + balcony

A 9′ glass slider opens onto a 4′ balcony under a 5′ roof overhang. Winter sun comes in and warms the room; summer sun is blocked. Your work desk sits under an inner glass panel that borrows morning light from the stair hall.

Kitchen · SE

Open kitchen, cook faces east

The kitchen is back in the SE, Vastu's best spot: an 11′ × 14′ kitchen, ⅓ walled, ⅔ open to the hall (the wall hides the hob and sink from the front door; the shoe cabinet sits on its foyer side) with an L-counter, the hob on the east counter, the sink at the NE end, fridge and pantry on the west. Its door opens onto the centre, near the dining.

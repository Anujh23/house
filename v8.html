Bhai's plan with the front half flipped, so the road, gate, sit-out and main door are on the east. The back half stays as drawn: kitchen SE, parents' bedroom SW, stair S, master upstairs over SW. Plot assumed the same size as bhai's (57′ × 43′).

How the D was made to sit right

  1. ✓ D on the north, door on the front. North glass gets light without the sun's heat. The main door is a plain solid teak door, so it feels like a proper main door and nobody at the door looks into the hall.
  2. ✓ No square glass beside the D. It is the only opening in the hall's north wall, with 1′ of solid wall at each end.
  3. ✓ One line at 7′. The main door, the D's door bar and the front windows all stop at 7′.
  4. ✓ A real sleeping-D: 14′8″ wide × 9′ tall, close to v7's 17′ × 10′, with teak lining through the wall inside and out.
  5. ? The D looks onto the 3′3″ north strip, so from inside you see the boundary wall and sky. Creepers or a painted boundary wall there make the view. The store shrinks to 5′6″ deep because the sit-out now sits where its front was.
